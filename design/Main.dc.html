GreenplateAi Crew puts six AI agents on your Meta glasses. Say "crew" and ask: what's missing for the week, move Tuesday's dinner, save this recipe. The crew works inside your GreenplateAi kitchen and answers in your ear.

A lead hands the work to a shopper, a pantry keeper, a planner, a logger and a chef. When a question needs more than one of them, they work at the same time.

How it helps

Hands-freeAsk while you cook or shop. Answers play in the glasses' speakers.
You stay in chargeEvery change to your plan, pantry or log waits for your yes.
Smarter shopping tripsItems are checked off as you pick them up, and you hear when something is already at home.

App privacy

The camera is only used during a shopping trip. Each frame is used to name the item in your hand, then dropped. No photos or video are stored.
