# Tuesday Nov 3: tests and the video

Submission closes Wed Nov 4, 9 PM ET. Each test ends as a line in the eval results, measured, not guessed.

## 1. Vision accuracy (about 45 min, mostly photos)
- You: photograph 25 things from the pantry and fridge with the glasses (Kitchen mode), one at a time.
  Say each one's real name out loud right after, so the log has the answer.
- Me: a scorer that runs every photo through Muse Spark vision and grades it: right item, right but
  vague ("pasta" for spaghetti), wrong, or "couldn't tell". Report: % right, typical time.

## 2. Wake phrase accuracy (about 15 min)
- You: say "Hey Crew, what's on my list?" 20 times in normal places (kitchen, TV on, walking),
  then talk normally for 10 minutes without saying it.
- Me: score from the service log: woke / missed / false wakes, and time from your last word to the
  answer starting.

## 3. Calorie estimates against real labels (about 20 min)
- You: 10 packaged foods. Photo of each nutrition label, and say "I had one serving of <name>".
- Me: compare GreenplateAi's logged estimate with the label. Report the typical miss in calories.
  Logged to a test day, then removed.

  handle keys). First check which models Meta's API already offers on our key.
